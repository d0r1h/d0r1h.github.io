Shlokam

A dataset of Sanskrit shlokas with English translations

Sanskrit NLP Translation Low-Resource
🤗 Hugging Face

Overview

Shlokam is a curated dataset of Sanskrit shlokas (verses) paired with their English translations. This dataset is designed for NLP research in low-resource languages, machine translation, and cultural heritage preservation.
